Date added, a Pacific day, stored as its start in UTC. A run warns (a count) when the timeline's one "Shelved" row has another day (0 of 1458).

Best effort: close to Goodreads' "date updated" (the list's sort key), not exactly it; never proof of a change. The book page's updatedAt when present (774/1458, to the ms); else the latest dated row of the entry's own timeline (a day). Against the RSS of 2026-10-27: same day on 732/770 and 656/677. The file's order is the exact signal of what changed last.
userReadCountnumberreview3implemented
One group with first and last read, per entry: its own "Finished Reading" rows, dated or not (rows ending "(… Edition)" are another entry's). The Great Code: 1, no dates.
